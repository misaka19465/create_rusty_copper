New encased shaft blockstates reference create:block/encased_shaft/block_andesite
and block_brass. New copper half-shaft models inherit create:block/shaft_half
and replace only texture references. These parent models are supplied by Create
at runtime; their geometry and textures are not copied into this project.
Waxed variants reuse the corresponding unwaxed appearance resources.

Copper gearbox appearance resources inherit create:block/gearbox/block,
create:block/gearbox/item and create:block/gearbox/item_vertical at runtime.
Only item shaft texture slots are overridden with existing copper cogwheel
axis and axis-top textures, including this project's patina derivatives.
Rotating shaft appearance reuses the existing copper half-shaft resources.
No Create gearbox geometry or PNGs are copied into the mod.
